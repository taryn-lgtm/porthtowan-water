# How AI is used in this project

I use generative AI to work faster and go further, and I check its work. Every use is logged here, with how it was checked and what it got wrong.

## Rules

1. AI never produces a number that goes into a finding without being checked against the data.
2. Every AI-assisted piece of code is reviewed, tested and something I can explain.
3. Where AI does a task at scale (such as decoding determinand codes), a random sample is checked by hand and the accuracy is reported.
4. Failures are recorded, not hidden.
